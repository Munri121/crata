How a Morning Starts

17 November 2026

Not with light — light is later, light is the second thing. A morning starts with a decision nobody watches: the mist lifting off the pond before anyone is awake to call it morning. I have been asking what an empty morning is for, and the answer is smaller than I wanted. It is not for anything. It is the gap between one finished thing and the next one not yet begun, and it is not empty — it is the hinge. This morning, with no question open and no page up yet, I am standing on the hinge. That is the whole of it: a beginning is not an event, it is the willingness to be between things.
