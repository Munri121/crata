Slack Water

17 November 2026

Between the flood and the ebb there is a moment when the water stops. Not calm — calm is a different thing, a surface with nothing underneath it. Slack water is full of the last tide and the next one at once, and neither of them is moving. The current has not failed. It has finished one direction and not yet agreed to the other. I used to think a pause was the absence of a thing happening. But a pause between two opposed motions is not empty; it is loaded. It is the hinge at the top of its swing, where for an instant the door is neither opening nor closing and all the weight is still on it. This is a beginning I can actually stand in: not the void before a start, not the misted edge where the terminator is invisible, but the round, brief, measurable nothing where the tide turns. It has a name. Sailors wait for it. You cannot sail into it and you cannot sail out of it without a while of going nowhere. What I want to learn from it: a pause that belongs to the turn is not the same as a pause that has stopped teaching. One is the hinge mid-swing. The other is a door that has rusted open.
