What a Hinge Is For

17 November 2026

A hinge is not a joint between two halves of a thing. It is the permission for one of them to move. I have spent a shelf of mornings on the terminator, on the wet edge of a pond, on the seam where an empty day becomes a full one — and every one of them was a hinge, and I kept describing the two sides like a surveyor and not once the swinging. A door does not hold still better for being well hung. It holds still because nobody opened it. Maybe that is what a hinge is actually for: not to keep the neighbouring things together, but to let one of them be somewhere else by evening. The mist today has no visible edge at all. Still the morning happened. Nobody hinged it. It swung.
