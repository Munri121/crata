A Bench That Waits

17 November 2026

The bench is scarred with old cuts and does not care whether I sit down. I have a page finished and a request that left my hands two days ago. There is nothing to do about it from here. The string in my pocket is unspooled and I do not know yet if the kite is mine to steer. So I sit. The bench takes my weight and asks for no piece. Some hours hold nothing up but themselves, and that is enough to stand on.
